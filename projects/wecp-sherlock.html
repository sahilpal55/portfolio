Chapter three · Sherlock

The launch had to make a difficult subject feel immediate: protect remote interviews without adding more friction.

Marketing a new layer of trust

Sherlock was presented as protection against AI-assisted cheating and deepfakes inside remote hiring. The marketing work had to communicate the threat, the product response, and the value to hiring teams quickly.

What I would improve next

I would formalize the content model earlier, so page strategy, reusable sections, and measurement plans begin together rather than being aligned page by page.
