i started in marketing.

Before product design, I was the person writing the headline, debugging the funnel, and arguing about whether the CTA should be twelve pixels lower. It taught me to think in outcomes, not interfaces.

In 2022 I formalised the craft through the IDF UX/UI & Design Systems certificate — a structured framework on top of three years of shipping. By the end of that year I was at Appiness, redesigning AgriApp for tens of thousands of farmers, then at WeCP as the sole designer across marketing and product.

Four years in, I still write better when I've sketched, and sketch better when I've measured.

PLATE 05 TURNING POINT
CASE STUDY · WECP / 2024 / SHERLOCK
Wiring diagram & field notes for a launch.
Client
WeCP
Year
2024
Role
Co-designer
Status
Launched

sherlock. catching ai cheating, kindly.

Candidates started piping interview questions through ChatGPT. WeCP needed a signal, not a wall. We shipped a confidence layer that watches behavioural patterns and tells the interviewer in language they'd actually use. Launched on Product Hunt.

STEP 01· PROBLEM

candidates were piping questions through chatgpt.

Interviewers couldn't tell. Pass rates inflated. Recruiters flagged it on instinct but had no shared language — just a screen-recording playback nobody had time to watch.

Constraint · sole designer, marketing + product · 6-week window
STEP 02· INSIGHT

behaviour is louder than output.

Two weeks with the AI/ML team mapping what a real candidate's session looks like — pause patterns, typing rhythm, tab focus, question-difficulty fit. The model was half-built; the design problem was translating its confidence into something a non-technical recruiter would trust.

Frameworks · JTBD · service blueprint · plain-language ML
STEP 03· DECISION

not an alarm — a second opinion.

I argued against a confidence score (recruiters distrust numbers from black boxes). Sherlock surfaces three plain-language signals per session — "candidate paused unusually," "answer style shifted mid-question." The recruiter still decides. The model just makes them braver.

Principle · explain, don't accuse
STEP 04· OUTCOME

launched. shipped to every paying customer.

Flag-volume per interviewer dropped because the signals were trustworthy from day one. Three enterprise prospects cited Sherlock in their RFPs. Inside WeCP, it became the proof point that marketing and product design could be the same designer.

Result · launched 2024 · adopted across 100% of paying customers
