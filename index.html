04 / About

Marketer who learned design

04+

I started in marketing: social media at Pencilton and Hypercube, digital marketing at Webware.io. While doing that, I taught myself design and earned a UX/UI certification from the Interaction Design Foundation.

I built my own portfolio to prove the skill. It got me my first design contract with MemoNeet, then Appiness, then WeCP, and now MAV Consulting.

Today I work end-to-end: discovery, high-fidelity UI, design systems, Webflow or Framer builds. AI tools like Figma Make and Google Stitch are part of the process, not a substitute for it.
