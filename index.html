About

Marketer who learned design

I started in marketing: social media management at Pencilton and Hypercube, digital marketing at Webware.io. While doing that, I taught myself design, then earned a UX/UI design certification from the Interaction Design Foundation.

I built my own portfolio to prove the skill, and it got me my first design gig: a freelance contract with MemoNeet. From there came Appiness, then WeCP, and now MAV Consulting.

Today I work end-to-end: discovery, high-fidelity UI, design systems, and Webflow or Framer builds. I use AI tools like Figma Make and Google Stitch inside the design process, not as a substitute for it.
